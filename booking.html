First consultation · 90 minutes

In this session, where we lay the foundations of our work, I listen to your full health history in detail and we begin your process with the indicated simillimum remedy. 

Between sessions · close follow-up via WhatsApp

In the way I work, following your process closely matters greatly. I ask for a short update over WhatsApp every two weeks. In acute situations — as long as it does not conflict with your remedy plan — I offer homeopathic support. In medical emergencies, always contact your doctor first

Follow-up consultations · 60–75 minutes

We meet roughly every 6–7 weeks (sometimes every two months). These are not brief check-ins; they are full sessions where we review the whole process again in detail — including the next layers and miasmatic blocks — and decide on the follow-up remedies.

Process and frequency

So you can plan ahead: over a 7–8 month homeopathic process we meet 4–5 times on average, reviewing your remedies each time. Meeting frequency and timing can be flexible from person to person.
