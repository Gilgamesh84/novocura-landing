The practice

A practice of attention, not of throughput

Most appointments are built around the clock. This one is built around you. A first consultation at Novocura runs to ninety minutes, and it needs to: your history, your sleep, your temperament, the small things that never fit into a form — all of it is part of the picture Hande works from.

Classical homeopathy treats the case as a whole rather than chasing single complaints. That way of working asks for time, quiet, and meticulous study between sessions. It is not the fastest model of care. It is, deliberately, the most attentive one.

“The consultation is not a preliminary to the work. The consultation is the work.”

Novocura is a complementary practice and works alongside, never instead of, your medical care. Nothing here asks you to choose between your doctor and your homeopath.

About

Hande listens for a living

Novocura began with a simple dissatisfaction: that most consultations end just as the real story starts to surface. Hande trained in classical homeopathy to practise differently — longer appointments, fewer patients, and case analysis given the hours it actually requires.

Her practice in Edinburgh is quiet by design. Many of the women who come to Novocura arrive after years of feeling rushed elsewhere; the first thing they notice is that nobody here is watching the clock.

Consultations are held in person in Edinburgh and online, in English or Turkish.

— Hande

What actually happens in a first consultation

keep scrolling — slowly
01 — LISTENING

Ninety minutes, unhurried

A first consultation begins with time. Your history, in your own words — not a form, not a checklist. Hande listens for the details that standard intake questions miss.

02 — CASE-TAKING

The whole picture, mapped

Sleep, appetite, temperament, history, the things that seem unrelated. Each detail is recorded with care — in homeopathy, the pattern matters more than any single complaint.

03 — ANALYSIS

The meticulous work between sessions

After you leave, the case is studied. Hande works through the notes against years of clinical training and materia medica — this is where the practice earns its name.

04 — FOLLOW-UP

Reviewed, refined, continued

A few weeks later, you sit down again. What has shifted, what hasn't, what that tells us. The work continues at the pace the case asks for — never rushed, never abandoned.

Practical matters

Before you book

WHERE

Edinburgh, and online

Consultations take place in a quiet room in Edinburgh, or by video call if you live further afield. The conversation is the same either way.

HOW LONG

Ninety minutes, first time

First consultations run to ninety minutes; follow-ups are shorter. You will never be asked to compress your story into a ten-minute slot.

HOW IT WORKS

Alongside your doctor

Homeopathy at Novocura is complementary care. Hande will always encourage you to keep your GP and specialists informed, and never to stop prescribed treatment.
