Who Am I?

Since 2008, I have worked mostly with complex cases and what we call multi-miasmatic chronic conditions. These are the difficult stories referred to me by colleagues, and the clients who fall outside the scope of healthy cases requiring a single remedy. That is why I moved beyond the classical homeopathic approach, towards Eizayaga's layer method, remedy maps, and a much deeper clinical perspective. I am so glad I did. Because in the end, I arrived back where I started: the Organon.

When I stepped into this profession in 2008, at just 26, homeopathy was a newly sprouting field in Turkey. At that age I could not have known how much labour, devotion and care the road would ask — and just as well I didn't. That thorny yet deeply transformative journey is still my greatest passion today.

Throughout my training I completed diploma education in three different countries, made clinical observations, and worked with many valuable teachers. While completing my first diploma, I took part, together with my colleagues, in founding the Turkish Homeopathy Association, where I would later serve as vice president. In 2018 I served as an educator at the Turkey branch of the Centre for Homeopathic Education. Today, in my own clinic in Scotland, I serve clients from Edinburgh and all over the world, and offer supervision and advanced trainings to homeopathy students and homeopaths.

This is the area of service where I build a system step by step for each client, and where my meticulous nature finds its place.

At the end of all this journey, I arrived where everything began: Hahnemann and the Organon. This is one of the things I am proudest of — the principles of homeopathy have barely changed in nearly three centuries. The world changed, diseases changed; but the principles Hahnemann set out centuries ago are still the compass of my work today. Few approaches in the world have roots this deep and this whole. I consider everyone who has encountered this approach — and myself — very fortunate.

And heartfelt thanks to the thousands of clients who have trusted me on this road.

— Hande

The Anatomy of a Session

1
LISTENING

Unhurried 

I ask you to tell your story in your own words — in a 90-minute session we translate your language into the language of homeopathy to reach the right remedy.

2
THE BODY'S LANGUAGE

Physical Signs

"I used to feel cold, now I'm burning", "spicy food bothers my stomach now", "my head aches on one side only, and I wake at 3 a.m. every night"... We watch the vital force speak through the body — and the body does not lie.

3
MIND AND EMOTION

Mental & Emotional Signs

Your temperament, your worries, your joys, your recurring patterns — without allowing speculation, we talk through the emotional states and predispositions that form the ground of your complaints.

4
ROOTS AND MIASM

What your family's story brings

Tendencies passed down through generations and your family's health patterns are part of the story too — in homeopathic understanding this is called the 'miasm'.
