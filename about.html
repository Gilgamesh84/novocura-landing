Hello,
I'm Hande.

Founder of Novocura Homeopathy · Homeopathy consultant and educator

When I stepped into this profession in 2008, at just 26, homeopathy was a newly sprouting field in Turkey. At that age I could not fully have known how much labour, devotion and care the road would ask — and just as well I didn't. That thorny yet deeply transformative journey is today my greatest professional wealth.

Eighteen years on, homeopathy is now a source of joy for me. Wrestling with complex stories and unanswered questions, I still carry the curiosity of the first day; in this method that opens a new door onto the person and their story every day, my apprenticeship never ends. I believe my people-centred, detail-loving, meticulous and intuitive temperament has been my greatest companion on this road.

Throughout my training I made clinical observations in three different countries and worked with many valuable teachers. While completing my first diploma, I took part in founding the Turkish Homeopathy Association with my colleagues. In 2018 I served as an educator at the Turkey branch of the Centre for Homeopathic Education. Today, in my own clinic in Scotland, I serve clients from Edinburgh and all over the world, and offer supervision and advanced trainings to homeopathy students and homeopaths.

“The principles of homeopathy have barely changed in nearly three centuries — the world changed; the principles remained our compass.”

At the end of all this journey, I arrived where everything began: Hahnemann and the Organon. This is one of the things I am proudest of — the principles of homeopathy have barely changed in nearly three centuries. The world changed, diseases changed; but the principles Hahnemann set out centuries ago are still the compass of my work today. Few approaches in the world have roots this deep and this whole. I consider everyone who has encountered this approach — and myself — very fortunate.

And heartfelt thanks to the thousands of clients who have trusted me on this road.

— Hande
